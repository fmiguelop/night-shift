# In-game controls

Scope: office door/light buttons and Help controls; Operate mode inside the established horror experience. User explicitly excludes camera buttons; preserve the camera map and monitor toggle.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Replace detached generic boxes with a pair of compact dark switch plates that belong beside the office doorways. Read a threat and act without a decorative interface competing with the room.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black plate, restrained warm-gray seams, Courier labels. Explicit OPEN/CLOSED and OFF/ON readouts. Red belongs to closed doors; an illuminated off-white face identifies a lit hall. No pictogram glyphs or new art.

STORY: Identify west/east, toggle door or light, read the resulting state immediately. Preserve A/D/Q/E shortcuts, power costs and camera-mode restrictions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Existing edge positions remain. Each plate groups a side label and two same-order Door/Light switches, with subdued key hints and visible state text. Help is a small text action that echoes the title-menu pointer. Camera controls remain unchanged.

FORM: Narrow code-led extension of the approved dark menu system. No new composition or concept round. Minimum 44px targets, readable active states and keyboard focus; check desktop and phone office/camera views.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
