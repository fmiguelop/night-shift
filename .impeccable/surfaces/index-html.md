# Main menu

Scope: title menu in index.html; Experience mode. User-pinned FNAF 1 screenshot supplies composition, not game branding or character artwork. Existing gameplay is outside scope.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A haunted title screen: three quiet title lines and bare actions beside Walter's enormous, half-obscured face. Remove the office-backed introduction and explanatory menu copy.

OWN-WORLD: Black, white and dirty gray. Courier typography, unboxed text actions and a double-chevron hover/focus marker. Existing Walter portrait under live television noise; no new character assets.

STORY: Start Night 1, continue the unlocked night, or quietly change sound/subtitles. Unlocks survive New Game. Night selection appears only after two completed nights (unlockedNight >= 3).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title near upper-left, New Game/Continue around mid-left, selected night underneath Continue. Walter fills the right two thirds. Settings anchors bottom-left with an upward panel. Native replay selector lives beneath the actions when eligible.

FORM: Direct code-led implementation of the user's pinned composition; no concept tournament. Signature interaction is throttled canvas interference, stopped during play and static with reduced motion. No generated comp.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
