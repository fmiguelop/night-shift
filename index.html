Your shift, explained.

Survive four real minutes to reach 6 AM. Characters move through the building toward your office.

Use Space for cameras. Click rooms on the map to track movement. Use Q / E to light the west / east hallway and reveal visitors. Toggle doors with A / D before visitors enter.

Closed doors, lights, and cameras consume extra power. Blocked characters retreat after a few seconds. At zero power, your doors open and you have only moments left.

The game pauses while these instructions are open.
