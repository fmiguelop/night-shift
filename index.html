Your shift, explained.

Survive four real minutes to reach 6 AM. Characters move through the building toward your office.

Use Space for cameras. Click rooms on the map to track movement. Use Q / E to light the west / east hallway and reveal visitors. Toggle doors with A / D before visitors enter.

Closed doors, lights, and cameras consume extra power. Blocked characters retreat after a few seconds. At zero power, your doors open and you have only moments left.

Walter: Heavy footsteps warn of his west approach. He moves faster while you use cameras.

Pip (after 35 seconds): Check the archives (CAM 06) to slow his tapping buildup. Running means close the west door immediately.

Dot (after 70 seconds): An eerie whistle and beak clicks announce her east approach. Short east-light flashes repel her; a held beam lets her adapt. Use the door if she stays.

Enable or disable subtitles in Settings on the main menu. When enabled, subtitles show directional cues even with sound off.

The game pauses while these instructions are open.
